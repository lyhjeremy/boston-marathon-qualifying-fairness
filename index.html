Boston BQ fairness 2026 standards · 22 brackets · 3 frameworks

Fair to women on average. Uneven bracket to bracket.

I scored every 2026 Boston Marathon qualifying standard against the bracket's world record, an averaged top three and WMA age grading. Men and women are asked for the same mean effort (Welch t-test, p = 0.81). The women's standards are spread about 3.5 times as widely (CV 6.6% against 1.9%), and W80+ is the strictest bracket in the table by a wide margin.

How it was tested

The BAA has never published how it sets these times, so fairness has to be measured against a reference. Each framework asks whether every bracket sits the same distance from its reference. A Welch t-test compares the gender means; Levene's test and the coefficient of variation compare the spread (Levene p = 0.036).

The variance gap holds under all three frameworks and under alternative reference records. Dropping W80+ alone cuts the women's CV from 6.6% to 4.5%. The 2026 cut of five minutes for everyone under 60 left the pattern in place and made the step at age 60 grow from 15 to 20 minutes.
